- The new message must be exactly: `I am excited to talk to you.`

## What it means
- The bot is being built by printing multiple messages in order.
- Each `console.log()` creates a new line of output in the console.
- The goal is to make the bot say two separate things:
  1. `Hi there!`
  2. `I am excited to talk to you.`

## Most important things to know
- Use `console.log("I am excited to talk to you.")`.
- The message must match the required text exactly.
- Do not remove the first `console.log()` statement.
