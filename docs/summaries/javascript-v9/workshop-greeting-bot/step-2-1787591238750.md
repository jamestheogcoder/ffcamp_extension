## What the topic is explaining
- This step introduces printing a second message from a simple greeting bot.
- It uses JavaScript’s `console.log()` function to display text in the console.
- The bot’s “conversation” is built by adding multiple `console.log()` statements.

## What the MCQs are asking
- There are no MCQs on this page.
- The exercise asks you to add another `console.log()` statement.
- The required output is the exact message: `"I am excited to talk to you."`

## What it means
- The bot is not truly “talking”; it is just printing messages in order.
- Each `console.log()` line adds one line of output.
- The goal is to make the bot appear to respond by showing a sequence of messages.

## Most important things to know
- Use `console.log("I am excited to talk to you.")` exactly.
- The message must match the required text, including punctuation.
- Adding another `console.log()` after the first message creates a second bot message.
